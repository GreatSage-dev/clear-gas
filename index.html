• CORE MECHANISMS

Three surgical components.
One atomic clearing engine.

⚡

EIP-2612 Atomic Permit

Users sign a gasless permit off-chain. The paymaster unpacks it directly inside validatePaymasterUserOp — no pre-approval transaction, no ETH, no wallet popup. One signature does everything.

🛡

Pyth Tri-State Oracle Guard

Every gas conversion is priced through Pyth's confidence-interval oracle. When market spread exceeds 150 bps, the engine enters epistemic quarantine — it refuses to guess, protecting user funds from volatile mispricing.

🔄

Self-Healing Buffer

The paymaster monitors its own EntryPoint deposit and auto-refills from its native ETH buffer when balance drops below threshold. No human operator needed. No AA21 reverts. Ever.
